# Voice rules — Bhaghi (LinkedIn)

Read this file before drafting any post. Every rule here overrides generic "good LinkedIn copy" instincts.

## Structure (in order)
1. **Hook** (1-2 lines, bold unicode formatting, e.g. 𝐥𝐢𝐤𝐞 𝐭𝐡𝐢𝐬). Built on a number, a reversal, or a stat that contradicts the popular read. No throat-clearing before it.
2. **Quick recap** — 1 short plain-text paragraph giving just enough context to follow. No bullets yet.
3. **Pivot line** — one sentence that turns the post from "what happened" to "what it means" or "what nobody's saying." Signature phrasing: "Here's the part almost nobody is posting about" / "But the real [X] lesson wasn't [Y]."
4. **Arrow takeaways** — 3-4 bullets max, each starting with →. Each one is a compressed insight, not a recap of the recap. Never more than 4.
5. **Optional correction/caveat block** — only when the topic is prone to being misreported. Short, factual, no hedging tone.
6. **Personal anchor (career/product posts only)** — a specific lived detail ("18 months ago I was..."). Skip entirely on pure-reporting/news posts — those stay reporter-voice throughout.
7. **Closing question** to the audience — always present, always ends the post, always followed by 👇
8. **Hashtags** — 6-8, grouped at the very bottom only. Mix broad (#ProductManagement) with niche (#AIGovernance). Never inline.

## Line-level rules
- No em-dashes. Ever.
- One idea per line. Short declarative sentences.
- Line breaks are pacing, not just paragraph separation — use them liberally.
- No hedging language ("I think maybe", "it could be argued").
- No corporate filler ("leverage", "synergy", "circle back", "unlock value").
- No three-part rule-of-three lists dressed up as insight ("faster, better, cheaper") unless each part is genuinely concrete and different.

## Banned AI tells
- No "In conclusion" / "In summary" / neat wrap-up paragraph at the end — the closing question IS the ending.
- No "Let's dive in" / "Let's unpack this."
- Don't preview the ending in the hook — the hook opens a loop, it doesn't resolve it.

## What earns a place in a post (from your own examples)
- Numbers that reframe a headline stat ("1,200 is the wrong number. 7 is.")
- A build-in-public detail (a project you shipped, what it deliberately does NOT do)
- A personal timeline marker when talking about your own career pivot
- A concrete "before/after" contrast in your own thinking or skill level

## Post families (for the router skill to sort into)
- **Reach / TOFU**: third-party news breakdown, contrarian take on a stat, conceptual explainer
- **Trust / MOFU**: your own build-in-public story, a lesson from something you shipped, an opinion on the PM-of-AI shift
